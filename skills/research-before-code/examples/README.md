# Examples — research-before-code

> **Status: scaffolded, empty.** No worked example has been recorded yet.

## What belongs here

A complete research record for a real decision made under the
[`research-before-code`](../SKILL.md) skill: the question, the candidates found, what was checked in
each, the evidence that settled it, what was rejected and why, and the decision that followed.

The reference for the expected shape is
[`decision-records/matrix-template.md`](../../../decision-records/matrix-template.md). An example
here should be that template filled in with a real case, including the parts that went wrong — a
record with no dead ends is a record that has been tidied into uselessness.

## Rules

- No proprietary or client-identifying detail. Genericise the domain, keep the reasoning.
- Every claim graded: `claim_type`, `evidence_level`, `confidence`, `verified_at`.
- Repositories mentioned must exist in [`metadata/repositories.json`](../../../metadata/repositories.json)
  or be added to the seed list and fetched, never cited from memory.
- Record the license situation for anything adopted, especially `license: null` cases, which are
  `no-license-do-not-redistribute` regardless of quality.
